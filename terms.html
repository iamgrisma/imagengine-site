Terms of Service & Usage Quotas

ImageEngine is built as a high-performance edge acceleration layer and hosting crash shield. Review our clear quota model, caching guarantees, and acceptable use requirements below.

💡

Architectural Purpose: Edge Delivery & Bandwidth Shield

ImageEngine is NOT a database or a permanent file storage replacement. Your original media assets remain safely hosted on your origin servers. ImageEngine dynamically fetches, compresses into modern WebP/AVIF formats, and broadcasts them across 330+ global edge cities.

By offloading image encoding and heavy file delivery to our edge infrastructure, your web servers avoid memory exhaustion, CPU spikes, and bandwidth overage bills during high-traffic events.

HTTP/2 429 Too Many Requests
Retry-After: 86400
Content-Type: application/json

Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

Domain Ownership & Verification

All client domains must undergo verified onboarding. Tenants may only route assets belonging to their registered domain or approved subdomains. Routing unauthorized 3rd-party domains will result in instant origin revocation.

Prohibited Content

Tenants may not process imagery containing malware, phishing assets, CSAM, unauthorized copyrighted materials, or content that violates international laws. We reserve the right to immediately terminate access for abusive activities.

Automated Scraping & Stress Attacks

Intentionally sending randomized pseudo-parameters (e.g. ?w=1, ?w=2, ?w=3) to artificially bust cache and exhaust compute will trigger automatic IP blacklisting at our edge firewalls.

4

SLA & Service Availability

Our edge CDN network leverages Tier-1 infrastructure across Cloudflare Global Edge with a target 99.9% uptime SLA. In the rare event of localized edge maintenance or upstream origin timeouts, our architecture is built with end-to-end multi-region redundancy.
