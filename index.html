Engine Architecture

How ImageEngine Shields Your Infrastructure

Stop letting uncompressed, slow-loading images drain your server bandwidth and crash your website during viral surges.

🛡️

Zero-Hit Origin Protection

Once an asset is requested the first time, our global Cloudflare Edge locks it into cache with s-maxage=31536000, immutable. Millions of subsequent visitors receive the asset from regional SSD edge nodes without touching your server again.

Result: 98%+ Origin Offload
🚀

Dual Vector & Raster Pipeline

Powered by native libvips with SIMD acceleration. Automatically identifies SVG vectors for crisp font rendering, and compresses JPEG/PNG portraits into lightweight WebP headshots with top-face centering.

Average: ~3.8 KB WebP Payload
🏢

Multi-Tenant Isolation

Every organization gets an isolated namespace (/{tenant}/{subdomain}/{path}). Zero collision between apps, subdomains, or products. Subdomains resolve dynamically with zero code changes.

Structure: /{org}/{sub}/{path}.webp
